Five equal-weighted dimensions under mfu-health.v6, 24 drivers; bands <40 danger / 40–65 needs attention / ≥65 healthy; no threshold triggers automatic action (mfu-spec.md §5). Drivers, if asked. Attractiveness (§5.1): competition ratio, fill rate, registration yield, competition vs peers, applicant trend slope, intake-floor compliance. Relevance (§5.2): labour-market alignment, demand coverage, demographic resilience (North 18–22, adjusted by the international-intake share), strategic-sector alignment, posting-volume trend, salary premium. Capability (§5.3): publications per faculty, senior-rank share, research concentration — computed from the programme's own มคอ.2 lecturer table (plan D28); retirement horizon and student–faculty ratio were dropped because they need HR data. Curriculum Quality (§5.4): pedagogical fit, industry engagement, QA framework maturity, curriculum revision age. Internationalisation (§5.5, plan D27, 2026-09-07): international intake share, medium of instruction, formal partnerships, international co-authorship, faculty international-degree share — it publishes only when at least three of the five are populated, and its anchors were ratified for the pilot by the project lead on 2026-09-08 (D35) with Academic Council ratification still pending. Lifecycle (§6): baseline 50 adjusted by seven capped signals, about ±110 of movement, bands <45 Sunset / 45–65 Stable / 65–80 Rising / ≥80 with content_modernity FRONTIER; three gates that can only downgrade. §6.2 states openly that making the AI signals dominant is a value judgement. Risk (§7): likelihood from recruitment decay, AI disruption and demographic pressure; the impact axis is only a gross-revenue estimate, so §7 and §11 recommend publishing likelihood and withholding the 5×5 matrix. The rules: §9 rules 1–5 — missing is excluded from the average, never 0 or 50; every score carries dataCompletenessPct; historical rows keep their methodology stamp; only complete periods are scored; n ≥ 5 on every job-board aggregate; the fallback tier is recorded whenever a cascade resolves a driver.

The eight gates are mfu-spec.md §12.1 in full: a provenance drawer on every chart carrying sources, freshness, method, version and caveats; confidence plus clickable sources on every AI output; a freshness timestamp on every data point; an audit log on every mutation and an inference log on every AI call; a methodology version stamped on every metric with the docs versioned in git; reconciled source data with a visible fetch date — the 2026-09-04 decision that replaced the blanket 30-day soak, keeping 30 days of green collector history for the job boards; and MFU DPO sign-off on the PDPA posture including the job-board projection. The limitations are mfu-spec.md §11, and each names the integration that would unblock it: financial sustainability needs Finance; graduate employment needs an alumni survey; student satisfaction needs the evaluation system; staff retirement needs HR; on-time graduation needs the Registrar. Internationalisation carries its own exclusions: inbound and outbound mobility counts, the nationality of teaching staff, and the rankings' institution-wide "international outlook" sub-score. AI governance if asked (§8): every prompt is a versioned code file with a Zod output schema, a behaviour change is a version bump, a published version is never edited in place, and outputs below 0.85 confidence go to an analyst review queue rather than straight to a score.
