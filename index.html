What it is

A standalone system that turns MFU's own documents into a Council-ready brief

1 · It reads what MFU already publishes

The approved มคอ.2 of each programme — courses, credits, learning outcomes, teaching methods, QA framework, the lecturer table — extracted by AI with a verbatim quotation behind every claim.

2 · It joins public evidence

Admissions and intake plans, government and aggregator job postings, OpenAlex publications, NESDC/DOPA demographics for the North. No commercial licence, no partner credential.

3 · It scores and explains

Five dimensions, a lifecycle verdict and a risk likelihood per programme — a dashboard a Dean reads in five minutes, an executive cockpit, and a Council pack PDF for governing-body papers.

It integrates with no internal MFU system. That is a deliberate scope choice: the system could be built and validated before any data-sharing agreement with the Registrar, HR or Finance — and it carries no personal data from MFU's internal systems at all.

The model

Five dimensions, a verdict, and rules that stop the system flattering itself

Attractiveness

Are students choosing this programme? Competition ratio, fill rate, applicant trend, intake-floor compliance.

Relevance

Does what we teach match what employers advertise for? Plus the demographic ceiling of the North.

Capability

Can we deliver it? Publications per lecturer, senior-rank share, research concentration — from the มคอ.2 lecturer table.

Curriculum Quality

Is the curriculum sound? Teaching fit, industry engagement, QA framework maturity, revision age.

Internationalisation

MFU's own addition. Medium of instruction, named partnerships, international intake, co-authorship, foreign-degree faculty.

On top of the score

Lifecycle verdict — Sunset · Stable · Rising · Frontier, from seven signed signals with AI-readiness weighted heaviest, and downgrade-only gates that can never promote a programme.

Risk likelihood — recruitment decay, AI disruption, demographic pressure. Likelihood is published; the 5×5 impact matrix is deliberately withheld until real finance data exists.

Four rules, non-negotiable

  • Missing is not zero. A driver with no data is excluded from the average and shown as “—”, never substituted with 0 or 50. Every score carries a completeness percentage.
  • Every formula is versioned. A past score keeps the methodology stamp it was computed under, so old numbers stay interpretable.
  • n ≥ 5. No job-market cell smaller than five postings ever reaches a score.
  • Provenance on every chart — sources, fetch date, method, version, caveats.

Governance and trust

Eight gates a number must pass before Council sees it

Provenance draweron every chart: sources, freshness, method, version, caveats
Confidence + sourceson every AI output, clickable through to the evidence
Freshness timestampon every single data point
Audit logon every mutation of the record
Inference logon every AI call — cost, sources, confidence
Methodology versionstamped on every metric, documents versioned in git
Reconciled sourcewith a visible fetch date; nightly collectors need 30 days of green history
DPO sign-offon the PDPA posture, including the job-board projection

And the discipline of saying what it cannot answer. Stated on every Council-facing surface:

Is this programme financially sustainable? · Do our graduates get jobs? · Are students satisfied? · Will we lose the teaching team? · Are students graduating on time? — each needs an internal integration MFU has not yet made.
Advertised salary is a demand-side signal — what employers offer for a role. It must never be read as what MFU graduates earn. Internationalisation likewise cannot see student mobility counts or staff nationality.
Citations. The eight gates are mfu-spec.md §12.1 verbatim (provenance drawer; confidence and clickable sources; freshness timestamp; audit log on every mutation and inference log on every AI call; methodology version stamped, docs versioned in git; reconciled source data with a visible fetch date — the 2026-09-04 decision that replaced the blanket 30-day soak, with the 30-day green history kept for collected job-board sources; MFU DPO sign-off on the PDPA posture including the job-board projection). The limitations table and the salary warning: mfu-spec.md §11 — the salary misreading is described there as "the single most damaging misreading this system could produce". Internationalisation's named exclusions (mobility counts, staff nationality, rankings' international-outlook sub-score): §11 as amended by D27. AI governance detail if asked: §8 — every prompt is a versioned code file with a Zod output schema, a behaviour change is a version bump, a published version is never edited in place, and outputs below 0.85 confidence go to an analyst review queue rather than straight to a score.
